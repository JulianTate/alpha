# Strategy

Initial hypothesis: event-driven spillover through documented economic relationships. Candidate score is provisional and versioned (`spillover-v1`); sparse historical evidence is penalized. Evidence classes are FACT, INFERENCE, MODEL_ESTIMATE, or SPECULATION. A signal is a candidate, never a prediction or guarantee.

Supported storage is extensible to other event families and horizons, but this first operational slice only generates spillover candidates. Production strategy changes require a new strategy version and separate experiment.
